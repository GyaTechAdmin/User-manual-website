For institutes

How to sign up your institute

Create an account, verify your credentials, then list courses once you are approved.

Before you start: keep your registration certificate, accreditation proof, and authorized contact ready.

  1. 1

    Create your institute account

    Open Institute Sign Up. Enter the official name, a work email, and a password.

  2. 2

    Verify your email

    Open the one-time link within 24 hours to activate the account.

  3. 3

    Complete your profile

    Add address, affiliation, accreditation body, and an authorized contact.

  4. 4

    Upload verification documents

    PDF or JPG, under 10 MB each:

    • Institute registration certificate
    • Accreditation or affiliation proof
    • Authorized signatory ID
  5. 5

    Submit for review

    Reviews usually take 2 to 3 business days. You will get an email with the result.

  6. 6

    List your courses

    After approval, your dashboard unlocks course listing tools.

If documents were rejected, watch the institute tutorials.
