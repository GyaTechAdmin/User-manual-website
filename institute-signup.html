For institutes

Institute account and profile guide

Learn how to register your institute, manage its profile, add courses, and make it visible to students.

Before you start: keep your institute details, contact information, course information, and supporting documents ready.

  1. 1

    How to register your institute

    Create your institute account and prepare to receive student applications.

    Step 1 of 5 Video Guide

    How to Register Your Institute

    Create your institute account

    Register your institution on GetYourAdmission so you can build its profile and start receiving student applications.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Open Institute Sign Up: Start the registration process from the institute section.
    2. 2. Enter Institute Details: Provide the institution's official information accurately.
    3. 3. Add Contact Information: Use an active work email and authorized contact details.
    4. 4. Create Login Details: Set secure credentials for the institute account.
    5. 5. Review and Submit: Check the information before completing registration.

    Quick tip: Use official institute details that match your supporting records.

  2. 2

    Institute dashboard overview

    Learn where to find your profile, courses, applications, and account tools.

    Step 2 of 5 Video Guide

    Institute Dashboard Overview

    Navigate the institute dashboard

    Use the dashboard as the central place for maintaining your institute and managing its activity.

    Key areas to review:

    1. 1. Profile: Open and review the public-facing institute information.
    2. 2. Courses: Access the tools for creating and managing course listings.
    3. 3. Applications: Find student applications received through the platform.
    4. 4. Navigation: Move between the main dashboard sections and account tools.
    5. 5. Account Details: Keep institute contact and login information current.

    Quick tip: Review each dashboard section before adding courses or publishing your profile.

  3. 3

    How to edit institute details and profile

    Update your profile, contact information, and branding for prospective students.

    Step 3 of 5 Video Guide

    How to Edit Institute Details & Profile

    Keep your institute profile current

    Maintain accurate profile, contact, and branding information so students can understand and contact your institute.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Open Profile Settings: Go to the institute profile area from the dashboard.
    2. 2. Review Institute Details: Check the displayed name, description, and institutional information.
    3. 3. Update Contacts: Confirm that the phone, email, and other contact information are current.
    4. 4. Review Branding: Add or update the visual information used on the profile.
    5. 5. Save Changes: Review the updated profile before saving it.

    Quick tip: Keep contact details current so prospective students can reach the correct team.

  4. 4

    How to add courses to your profile

    Add and manage course offerings, fee information, and seat availability.

    Step 4 of 5 Video Guide

    How to Add Courses to Your Profile

    Publish accurate course information

    Create course listings that help students understand your programs, fees, and available seats.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Open Course Management: Go to the course section in the institute dashboard.
    2. 2. Add a Course: Start a new listing and enter the course information.
    3. 3. Add Fees: Provide the relevant fee details clearly.
    4. 4. Set Availability: Enter the current seat availability and other requested information.
    5. 5. Review and Save: Check the listing before adding it to your institute profile.

    Quick tip: Review course fees and seat availability regularly so students see current information.

  5. 5

    How your institute profile becomes public

    Complete your profile and courses, then follow verification for student visibility.

    Step 5 of 5 Video Guide

    How Your Institute Profile Becomes Public

    Prepare your profile for publication

    Complete the institute profile and course information, then follow the verification process that makes it visible to students.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Complete the Profile: Confirm that the institute information is accurate and complete.
    2. 2. Review Courses: Make sure the required course listings are ready.
    3. 3. Check Supporting Information: Confirm that requested records and contact details are available.
    4. 4. Follow Verification: Complete the platform's review requirements.
    5. 5. Confirm Visibility: Review the public profile once it becomes available to students.

    Quick tip: Review the public profile after approval to confirm that students see accurate information.
