How to create your student account

Sign up in minutes, verify your email or phone, and complete your profile so you can start browsing and applying to institutes.

Overview

Student sign-up is quick — no documents needed. Create an account, verify it, and fill in a short profile so we can match you with relevant institutes and courses.

Before you start: have your education details (current level and stream of interest) ready — it speeds up profile setup in step 3.

Step-by-step instructions

  • 1

    Register with email or phone

    Go to Student Sign Up and enter your name, and either an email address or phone number, plus a password.

  • 2

    Verify your account

    Enter the OTP sent to your email or phone. This confirms the account is really yours and unlocks the rest of sign-up.

  • 3

    Complete your student profile

    Add your current education level, subjects or streams you're interested in, and preferred locations. This helps us surface relevant institutes first.

  • 4

    Browse and save institutes

    Explore institutes and courses that match your profile. Tap the save icon on any listing to keep it in your shortlist for later.

  • 5

    Apply to your first program

    When you're ready, open a course page and hit Apply. You can track every application's status from your dashboard.

Need more help?

If you didn't receive your verification code or can't access your account, visit the Help & Support page for troubleshooting steps.
