For students

Student account and application guide

Learn how to create your account, apply to courses and institutes, upload documents, and manage your student dashboard.

Before you start: keep your education level and preferred stream ready.

  1. 1

    Register with email or phone

    Open Student Sign Up. Enter your name, an email or phone number, and a password.

    Step 1 of 5 Video Guide

    How to Register & Create an Account

    Brief Walkthrough & Instructions

    Watch the video above for a complete walkthrough of student registration. In this initial step, you establish your secure student credentials to unlock the admissions portal, search certified colleges, and submit program applications.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Access Sign Up: Click on Student Sign Up from the top navigation bar or the home screen.
    2. 2. Enter Basic Details: Type your full name as it appears on your official educational certificates.
    3. 3. Provide Email or Phone: Enter your active email address or mobile phone number to receive admission alerts and verification codes.
    4. 4. Set a Secure Password: Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols).
    5. 5. Agree & Submit: Check the agreement box to accept terms of service, then click Create Account to immediately trigger your verification code for Step 2.

    Quick tip: Double-check your mobile number and email before hitting submit so your verification OTP delivers without delay.

  2. 2

    How to apply for courses directly

    Browse available courses, review the details, and submit an application.

    Step 2 of 5 Video Guide

    How to Apply for Courses Directly

    Apply from a course listing

    Use the course listings to compare options and apply directly to the program that fits your goals.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Browse Courses: Open the available course listings and use the filters to narrow your choices.
    2. 2. Review Details: Check the course, institute, eligibility, fees, and other important information.
    3. 3. Choose a Course: Open the course you want and select its application option.
    4. 4. Confirm Your Information: Review your profile and provide any requested application details.
    5. 5. Submit and Track: Send the application and monitor its progress from your dashboard.

    Quick tip: Check the eligibility and required documents before submitting your application.

  3. 3

    How to apply to specific institutes

    Find an institute, review its profile and courses, then submit your application.

    Step 3 of 5 Video Guide

    How to Apply to Specific Institutes

    Apply from an institute profile

    Start with the institute you prefer, explore its available programs, and apply through the institute profile.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Search Institutes: Find the college or institute you want to apply to.
    2. 2. Open Its Profile: Review the institute overview and available information.
    3. 3. Explore Courses: Check the programs offered by that institute.
    4. 4. Select a Program: Open your preferred course and choose the application option.
    5. 5. Submit and Track: Confirm your details, submit the application, and follow its status from your dashboard.

    Quick tip: Review the institute profile and course requirements together before applying.

  4. 4

    How to upload documents and profile data

    Complete your student profile and add the documents needed for applications.

    Step 4 of 5 Video Guide

    How to Upload Documents & Profile Data

    Prepare your application profile

    Keep your personal, educational, and document information complete so institutes can review your applications.

    Key steps to follow:

    1. 1. Open Your Profile: Go to the profile section from your student account.
    2. 2. Add Profile Data: Complete the requested personal and educational information.
    3. 3. Prepare Documents: Use clear, readable files for the documents requested by your chosen programs.
    4. 4. Upload Each File: Add the correct document in its matching upload field.
    5. 5. Save and Review: Confirm that your information and uploaded files are complete and current.

    Quick tip: Use clearly named files and check that every document is readable before saving.

  5. 5

    Student dashboard overview and navigation

    Manage your profile, documents, submitted applications, and account in one place.

    Step 5 of 5 Video Guide

    Student Dashboard Overview & Navigation

    Use your student dashboard

    The dashboard gives you one place to maintain your account and follow your admission activity.

    Key areas to review:

    1. 1. Profile: Review and update your personal and educational information.
    2. 2. Applications: View submitted applications and check their current status.
    3. 3. Documents: Confirm which files are uploaded and replace outdated documents when needed.
    4. 4. Navigation: Use the dashboard sections to move between courses, institutes, and account tools.
    5. 5. Account Management: Keep your contact information and account details current.

    Quick tip: Check your dashboard regularly for application updates and missing information.

If you did not get a verification code, watch the student tutorials.
