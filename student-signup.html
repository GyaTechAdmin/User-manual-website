For students

How to create your student account

Register, verify your email or phone, then finish a short profile so you can apply.

Before you start: keep your education level and preferred stream ready.

  1. 1

    Register with email or phone

    Open Student Sign Up. Enter your name, an email or phone number, and a password.

  2. 2

    Verify your account

    Enter the code sent to you. This unlocks the rest of sign-up.

  3. 3

    Complete your profile

    Add your education level, subjects of interest, and preferred locations.

  4. 4

    Browse and save institutes

    Open listings that match your profile and save any you want to revisit.

  5. 5

    Apply to a program

    On a course page, choose Apply. Track status from your dashboard.

If you did not get a verification code, watch the student tutorials.
